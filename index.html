Three Chicken Franchises,
One Fair Comparison

Compare BBQ, bhc, and Kyochon using average annual sales per franchise location. / 가맹점당 연평균 매출액을 기준으로 BBQ, bhc, 교촌치킨을 비교합니다.

1. The brands / 브랜드 소개

BBQ

A Korean chicken brand known for its fried chicken and Golden Olive menu.

후라이드 치킨과 황금올리브 메뉴로 알려진 국내 치킨 브랜드입니다.

bhc Chicken

A large Korean chicken franchise known for menu lines such as Bburinkle.

뿌링클 등 다양한 메뉴로 알려진 국내 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

Kyochon Chicken

A Korean brand associated with soy-garlic and honey-style chicken.

간장·마늘 및 허니 스타일 치킨으로 잘 알려져 있습니다.

2. Comparison criteria / 비교 기준

Metric: average annual sales per franchise location. Base year: 2023. These are franchisee-store sales, not company-wide revenue or profit.지표: 가맹점당 연평균 매출액. 기준 연도: 2023년. 본사 전체 매출이나 순이익이 아닙니다.

The KFTC report was published in April 2025, but these figures refer to 2023 data. Values are in Korean won (KRW) and rounded where noted.

4. Search history / 검색 기록

Query 1 — General search

domestic franchise revenue rankings

Broad results can mix corporate revenue, franchisee sales, store counts, news and blogs. Check the metric carefully.

Query 2 — PDF documents

domestic franchise revenue rankings filetype:pdf

PDF reports may make official statistics and methodology easier to find, but can be harder to search inside.

Query 3 — Naver pages

domestic franchise revenue rankings site:naver.com

Limits results to Naver-hosted pages. Check both the article date and the data year.

Query 4 — Date range

domestic franchise revenue rankings after:2024-06-01 before:2026-01-01

Limits publication dates, but a new article may still use old data. Repeat the searches and add your own observations for the assignment.

These describe typical search strategies, not a saved record of every live result. Repeat each query and note what you personally find.

5. Conclusion / 결론

Based on the figures shown, Kyochon has the highest reported 2023 average franchise sales, followed by bhc. BBQ is approximate. Average sales are not profit.

제시된 수치 기준으로 교촌치킨의 2023년 가맹점당 평균 매출이 가장 높고 bhc가 그 뒤를 잇습니다. BBQ 수치는 근사치이며 평균 매출은 순이익이 아닙니다.
